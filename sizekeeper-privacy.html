Privacy Policy

SizeKeeper — Last updated October 2026
TL;DR — The short version

SizeKeeper stores all your data locally on your device. We don't collect it, sell it, or send it anywhere. Your sizes are yours.

What data SizeKeeper stores

SizeKeeper stores the following data locally on your device using your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB:

  • Names, relationships, and clothing sizes for people you add
  • Photos you add to profiles or capture for wishlist items (stored in IndexedDB on your device)
  • Barcode numbers read from items you scan (saved with that wishlist item, on your device)
  • The app's own files, cached by your browser so SizeKeeper works offline
  • Birthday dates and growth tracking entries
  • Fit notes, brand preferences, and wishlist items
  • App settings including your chosen color theme
  • Your early access email address (if you chose to provide one)

Where your data goes

Nowhere. All data is stored exclusively on your device. SizeKeeper does not have a backend server, does not transmit your data over the internet, and does not sync to any cloud service.

The only exception is the early access waitlist email — if you tap "Notify Me" and enter your email, that email is stored locally on your device. We do not currently transmit it anywhere. When we launch a notification system, we will update this policy and ask for your explicit consent before sending anything.

Third-party services

SizeKeeper loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). This means Google's servers receive a request for font files when you open the app. This is standard practice and Google's font API does not receive any of your personal data from SizeKeeper.

No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, no crash reporting services are included in SizeKeeper.

Camera

SizeKeeper only uses your camera when you tap to capture an item or scan a barcode. Camera images are processed on your device. Barcodes are read on your device, and the number is never looked up online or sent anywhere. You can deny camera access and still type items in by hand.

Photos

Photos you add to profiles or capture for wishlist items are stored in IndexedDB on your device. They are never uploaded to any server. If you uninstall the app or clear your browser storage, these photos will be deleted along with all other app data. We recommend using the Export function in Settings to back up your data.

Children's privacy

SizeKeeper may be used to store clothing sizes for children (for example, to track a child's growth over time). We do not collect any data from children or about children — all data stays on your device and is controlled entirely by you.

Data deletion

You can delete all SizeKeeper data at any time by going to Settings → Data → Reset App. This permanently removes all profiles, photos, and settings from your device. You can also delete individual profiles from within the app.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that involve data transmission (such as cloud sync or AI features requiring a server), we will update this policy before those features are available and will not collect any new data without your knowledge.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at privacy@sizekeeper.app

SizeKeeper is an independent app. This policy applies to the SizeKeeper app and any associated web properties.
